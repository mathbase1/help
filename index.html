I have forgotten my username or passwordAccount access

Email info@pemtutoring.co.uk. Include the learner username if it is known and enough information for the account holder to be verified.

  • Do not send the existing password or any temporary password by email.
  • Support may ask the parent or account holder to confirm account information before making changes.
  • A password reset should create a new secure password or a time-limited reset link; the existing password should never be revealed.
Security reminder: PEM Tutoring will not need to know the learner's current password to reset access.
What information is collected?Data and privacy

The service may hold the minimum information needed to operate the learner account and provide the learning service. This can include:

  • Account information: private username, securely protected password record, account status, access dates and subscription or entitlement details.
  • Learning information: practice progress, exam attempts, raw scores, percentages, timings, answers, topic breakdowns, feedback and reports.
  • Security information: sign-in and sign-out times, active session details, device and browser information, and limited network or location indicators used to protect the account.
  • Support information: messages sent to support and actions taken to resolve the request.
Data minimisation: parents and learners should avoid providing names or other personal details that are not required for the account or support request.
How is the information used?Data and privacy

Account and learning information is used to:

  • confirm that the learner is allowed to access the service;
  • save learning activity and completed exam records;
  • calculate scores, topic summaries and progress information;
  • manage account access, expiry, suspension, reactivation or deletion;
  • prevent unauthorised account sharing and simultaneous use;
  • investigate technical or security problems and respond to support requests; and
  • meet legal, accounting, safeguarding or security obligations where they apply.

Learner records are intended for practice and progress reporting. They are not a formal admissions result or a guaranteed prediction of 11+ success.

No third-party advertising: learner results should not be used to target third-party advertising.
What is saved from exams and practice?Results and feedback

A completed exam record may contain the date and time, subject, paper identifier, raw score, total marks, percentage, completion time, answers, correct answers, topic breakdown and stored feedback.

  • Completed exam attempts are shown in My Performance.
  • The performance history is intended to show change over time and identify topics that need more work.
  • Practice activity may save progress for continuity, but only completed exam records should populate the formal attempt history on the performance page.
  • Scores and summaries are calculated using programmed marking and arithmetic, not external AI analysis.
If a completed result is missing or appears incorrect, contact support with the username, subject, approximate completion time and paper identifier if available.
Data protection and your rightsData and privacy

Personal information must be handled in line with applicable UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Depending on the circumstances and the reason the information is being used, the account holder may be able to ask for access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, portability or an objection to certain uses.

  • Requests can be sent to info@pemtutoring.co.uk.
  • Identity and authority to act for the learner may need to be verified before account information is released or changed.
  • Some rights are not absolute and can depend on the lawful basis, legal obligations and the information concerned.
  • Individuals may also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.
Important: this help section is a summary and does not replace the full privacy notice.
How long is information kept?Data and privacy

Information should be kept only for as long as it is needed for account management, learning records, service security, support and any applicable legal obligations.

  • Active account records may be retained while the learner continues to use the service.
  • Security and support records may be kept for a limited period to investigate problems, prevent misuse or maintain an audit trail.
  • When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or anonymised in line with the retention schedule.
  • Deletion from live systems may not remove information immediately from protected backups; backup copies should expire through the normal retention cycle.
The formal privacy notice and retention schedule should state the exact periods that apply to each category of information.
Account sharing, active sessions and inactivityAccount security

Each username and password is for the authorised learner account and should not be shared with friends or extended family.

  • Only one active session should be permitted for an account at a time.
  • A simultaneous sign-in from another device or location may be blocked or may end the existing session.
  • After five minutes without activity, the service should ask whether the learner is still present.
  • If there is no response within the following two minutes, the session should end automatically.
  • An administrator may view whether an account is online or offline and may end an active session where necessary.
Keep credentials private: account sharing can prevent the authorised learner from signing in and may lead to account restrictions.
Suspended, reactivated or deleted accountsAccount access

A suspended account cannot be used to sign in. Under the current account policy, suspension clears the learner's saved quiz, exam, answer, feedback and progress records.

  • If the account is later reactivated, it starts again without the previous learning history.
  • Cleared learning data cannot be restored from the learner account.
  • Permanent deletion removes the account and access and is intended to be irreversible.
  • Limited administrative, legal, security or backup records may remain for the period required by the retention policy.
Before requesting suspension or deletion: download or record any results that need to be retained.
A page is not loading or working correctlyTechnical support

Try the following before contacting support:

  • Check the internet connection and refresh the page once.
  • Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  • Make sure JavaScript and essential cookies or browser storage are allowed.
  • Close unused tabs or applications if the device is running slowly.
  • Do not repeatedly submit an exam while the page is processing or saving.
  • If the problem continues, take a screenshot and note the page, browser, device and approximate time.
Recovery of unsaved work depends on whether the most recent response or result was successfully saved to the server.
Cookies and browser storageData and privacy

Essential cookies or similar browser storage may be used to keep the learner signed in, protect the session, remember necessary preferences and support continuity between pages.

  • Essential storage is required for core account and security functions.
  • Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies should be explained separately and used only where the required notice or consent has been provided.
  • Logging out should end the active session and remove or invalidate the relevant authentication data.
The cookie notice should identify the technologies actually used in the production service and their expiry periods.
How do I contact support?Technical and account support

Email info@pemtutoring.co.uk for account access, password resets, data requests, missing results or technical issues.

  • Use the learner username rather than the learner's full name where possible.
  • Explain what happened and what should have happened.
  • Include the page, subject or activity and the approximate date and time.
  • State the device and browser being used.
  • Do not include passwords, payment card details or unrelated personal information.
Account privacy: support may need to verify the parent or account holder before discussing or changing protected account information.

Developer notes — do not include on real pages

Production route and navigation: this file uses the established Dashboard, Practice, Exams, My Performance and prototype Log out destinations. The prototype Help route is https://mathbase1.github.io/help/ and is linked from every signed-in navigation bar. For production, replace prototype GitHub destinations with the authenticated production routes. Production logout must revoke the server-side session before returning to the production login route.

Data entering this page: the public help content should come from an approved, versioned help-content source or CMS. The support email, privacy-notice version, cookie-notice version, retention summary and service-status messages should come from central configuration rather than being duplicated across HTML files. The only learner-specific value this page may need is the authenticated account identifier for pre-filling a support ticket; it should not load detailed exam answers or results merely to display help content.

Account and learning data described by the page: the production backend may hold pseudonymous username, password hash, account type/status, access or subscription dates, exam and practice records, answers, marks, feedback, reports, session identifiers, login/logout times, last activity, device/browser information, limited IP or coarse-region security data, support tickets and an administrator audit trail. The formal privacy notice must accurately identify the controller, purposes, lawful bases, recipients/processors, international transfers, retention periods and rights that actually apply.

Password reset: implement a generic reset response that does not reveal whether an account exists. Use a cryptographically random, single-use, short-lived token; store only a hash of the token; invalidate previous tokens; rate-limit requests; record the security event; revoke existing sessions after a successful reset; and never email or display an existing password.

Support workflow: a support request should create a server-side ticket linked to the authenticated username or a verified parent/account-holder identity. Store the category, message, page, timestamp, device/browser and attachments only where necessary. Strip hidden metadata where appropriate, restrict staff access by role, scan uploads, keep a complete action history and apply a documented retention period.

Privacy requests: provide a protected workflow for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaints where applicable. Verify identity and authority to act for a child before disclosure or destructive action. Log the request, deadline, decision, data searched, response and any lawful reason for retaining information. This help page is not a substitute for a product-specific privacy notice, child-friendly privacy information, retention schedule or cookie notice.

Session and account-sharing controls: enforce the one-active-session rule on the server using a central session store. Track session ID, account ID, issued/last-activity/expiry times, device summary and revocation reason. After five minutes of inactivity, present an accessible “Are you still there?” dialog; allow two minutes for a response; then revoke the session server-side and redirect to login. A second login must be rejected or must revoke the earlier session according to the final policy. Administrators must be able to see online/offline state and force logout without exposing unnecessary location data.

Suspension, reactivation and deletion: the visible wording currently reflects the requested policy that suspension clears learner quiz, exam, answer, feedback and progress data and reactivation starts with no prior learning history. Before production, confirm this destructive policy against contractual, safeguarding, accounting, backup and data-protection requirements. Use transactional backend operations, explicit administrator confirmation, immutable audit events, backup-expiry rules and clear notice to the account holder. Permanent deletion must be separately authorised and should never rely on client-side JavaScript.

Cookies and security: use secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookies over HTTPS; CSRF protection; strong password hashing; rate limiting; account lockout controls; least-privilege roles; encryption in transit and at rest; secret management; database backups; monitoring; alerting; vulnerability patching; and a tested breach-response process. Only describe cookies and analytics that are actually deployed.

Content governance and accessibility: assign an owner and review date to every help article. Keep legal and operational text synchronised with the live product. Preserve the native details/summary keyboard behaviour, visible focus, live search status, reduced-motion support and responsive layout. Test with keyboard-only use, screen readers, 200%/400% zoom, iOS Safari, Android TalkBack, tablet layouts and managed school browsers before release.
